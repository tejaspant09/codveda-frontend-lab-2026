# Frontend Lab design system

Audience: internship evaluators, frontend learners, and recruiters. Use: try actual demos and inspect evidence. Tone: precise, restrained, technical. Fictional product: Still, a focus timer. No invented customers, endorsements, or outcome metrics.

Console theme, graphite colourway, from Hallmark deal 3/3. Shared tokens and base rules live in shared/. Use Host Grotesk for text and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono only for measurements/code. All colours and font families go through :root tokens; no literal colours in selectors. Accent amber belongs to controls, cool blue to readings. No gradients or shadows. Native accessible elements, 48px controls, instant focus rings, no horizontal overflow.

Landing: Workbench shape; Counter hero replaces dealt Corridor because the real timer is the action. Floating navigation, live timer at graphic scale, feature rows, a product usage preview, a short FAQ, letter-close footer. No price, testimonials, or marketing figures. Simple app pages use compact Banner heads. One primary button recipe, restrained borders, capped container. Animations are explicitly required by Level 3 and override Console's normally static motion: use one short timeline and one scroll reveal; honor reduced motion and keep content visible without JavaScript.

Root delivery hub: an index of the real projects and their task mapping. Each demo links back to / and adjacent tasks. Level 1 build base /level-1/. Level 2 app base /level-2/. Storybook /library/. Keep implementation and internship-policy commentary out of normal product flows, except accurate demo disclosures.
